About Me

I'm a PhD Candidate in Computer Science at the University of Bucharest, since October 2021, under the supervision of Ioana Leuștean.
I'm currently intrested in Logic, Artificial Intelligence and Computer Architecture.
I am also a .Net Developer and AI Engineer since November 2018.
